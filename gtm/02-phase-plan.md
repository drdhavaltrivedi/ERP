# Lead generation phase plan

Version 0.1, 13 Nov 2026.

## Phases

| Phase | Scope | Gate to move on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ICP (see `01-icp-odoo-mid-market.md`) | Your review and answers to the ICP questions |
| 2 | 100 accounts in Ahmedabad district, routed by industrial cluster for visits | You review the first 25 accounts for quality before the remaining 75 are built |
| 3 | 100 accounts in the rest of Gujarat (Vadodara, Surat, Ankleshwar, Vapi, Rajkot, Morbi, Mehsana, Bharuch) | Feedback from the first Ahmedabad conversations (which industries and signals actually converted) |
| 4 | 100 accounts across India (Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Coimbatore, NCR, Indore) | Same, plus a decision on remote vs. in-person selling |
| 5 | Pick an export market | Shortlist to evaluate later: UAE/GCC (VAT, many Indian-owned SMEs, short travel), East Africa (Kenya, Tanzania, strong Gujarati business diaspora), UK (Indian-owned manufacturing and distribution SMEs). To be assessed with data, not chosen now. |

## Spreadsheet columns (Phase 2 onward)

Account ID, Company, Industry, Sub-segment, Headcount band, Address, Cluster / area, Website, Phone (board), Google Maps link, Decision maker 1 (name, title, LinkedIn, source + date), Decision maker 2, Email (and verification status), Pain signal, Signal source URL, Current system (if known), Fit score, Tier, Owner (rep), Status, Next step, Notes.
