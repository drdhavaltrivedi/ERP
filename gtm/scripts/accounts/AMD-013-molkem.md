## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Ab kaunse market sabse tezi se badh raha hai: Brazil, Vietnam ya Middle East?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Chaaron countries ka hisaab alag currencies mein hai, toh group ka consolidated view kaise banta hai?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Sourcing aur purchase approvals abhi email aur WhatsApp par hote hain, ya kisi system mein?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Toh multi-country consolidation aur purchase approvals abhi Excel, email aur WhatsApp mein bikhre hue hain.

## 6. One line of value, specific to them (15 seconds)

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein India, Brazil, Vietnam aur Dubai multi-currency mein ek saath consolidated, aur purchase approvals system mein. Tally India GST ke liye reh sakta hai.

## 7. Ask for the visit (not a demo)

> **Dhaval:** Kya main next week Bodakdev office aa ke 30 minute mil sakta hoon? Main dikhaunga ki aapke jaisa setup ek system mein kaisa dikhta hai. Tuesday ya Thursday, kaunsa theek rahega?

*Always offer two options. When they agree:* "Main aapko WhatsApp pe confirm bhej deta hoon. Yeh number sahi hai?"

## Questions they will probably ask

- **Multiple plants, companies or countries?** Yes. Multi-warehouse, multi-company and multi-currency are standard in both. This is one of the main reasons to move off Tally.
- **Is our data safe? Who can see it?** Each user sees only what their role allows. You can host on a cloud server in India, or on your own server at the office. You own the data and can export it any time.
- **Why both Odoo and ERPNext? Isn't that confusing?** It lets us recommend what fits you instead of pushing one product. Odoo is more polished and covers more areas: CRM, website, quality, helpdesk. ERPNext is open source with no per-user licence, so it's cheaper to run and easier to customise. We tell you which one we'd pick for you, and why.
