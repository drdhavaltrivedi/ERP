## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Teeno plants mein se sabse bada production kahan hota hai, aur nayi line kis plant mein lag rahi hai?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Vatva, Khambhat aur Dahej ke beech stock transfer hota hai toh teeno jagah ka stock milta hai, ya month-end par mismatch aata hai?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Batch ka yield aur by-product ka costing plant-wise abhi kaise nikalte hain?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Toh inter-plant transfer aur plant-wise yield costing abhi Excel mein jud raha hai, aur nayi line aane se yeh aur complex hoga.

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein teeno plants ka stock transfer, batch yield aur by-product costing ek system mein, aur nayi line pehle din se usi structure mein. Tally accounts ke liye reh sakta hai.

## Questions they will probably ask

- **Chemicals: recipes, yield, by-products, multiple units of measure?** Yes. A bill of materials can produce by-products, and you can record actual yield against standard. You can buy in kg and sell in drums or tankers.
- **Multiple plants, companies or countries?** Yes. Multi-warehouse, multi-company and multi-currency are standard in both. This is one of the main reasons to move off Tally.
- **Will our business stop during the switch?** No. We run the new system alongside the old one for one or two weeks before go-live, so you can compare the numbers. Many clients go live on 1 April or at a month start, so the books open clean.
