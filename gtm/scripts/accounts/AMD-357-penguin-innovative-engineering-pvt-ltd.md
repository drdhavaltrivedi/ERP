## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Pani puri machine se lekar tea aur dosa machine tak range bahut badi hai, engineering team mein kitne log hain?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Machine ka BOM, sheet metal parts ka stock aur job work ka hisaab dono workshops mein kaise rakhte ho?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Machine ke spare parts aur warranty service ka record kaise rakhte ho, export customers ke liye bhi?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Agar BOM aur spare parts ka hisaab manual hai, to ek machine ki costing aur warranty part dono mein time lagta hai.

## 6. One line of value, specific to them (15 seconds)

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein machine ka BOM costing, job work aur spare parts ek saath chalenge, aur Tally GST aur accounts ke liye rehne de sakte ho.
