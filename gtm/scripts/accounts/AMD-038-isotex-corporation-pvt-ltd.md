## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Naroda aur Dahej dono plants mein heavy fabrication ka kaam kaise divide karte hain?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Boiler ka BOM aur project costing aaj kis system mein hota hai?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Spare parts aur AMC service ke liye 25+ service centres ka stock aur order kaise track karte hain?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Toh BOM aur project costing abhi manually ho rahi hai, isse har project ka hisaab aur delivery track karna mushkil hota hoga.

## 6. One line of value, specific to them (15 seconds)

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein Naroda aur Dahej ka BOM, project costing, spare parts aur AMC service ek jagah milenge; Tally accounts aur GST ke liye rahega.

## 7. Ask for the visit (not a demo)

> **Dhaval:** Kya main next week Naroda head office aa ke 30 minute mil sakta hoon? Main dikhaunga ki aapke jaisa setup ek system mein kaisa dikhta hai. Tuesday ya Thursday, kaunsa theek rahega?

*Always offer two options. When they agree:* "Main aapko WhatsApp pe confirm bhej deta hoon. Yeh number sahi hai?"

We give a fixed quote after a half-day discovery.
