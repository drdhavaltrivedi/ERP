## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Divine ka Chhatral plant, 8000 MT se upar capacity, aur seamless aur welded dono lines, team kitni badi hai?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "SS tube mein heat number aur material certificate ka batch-wise tracking, dispatch ke waqt, kaise hota hai?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Export ke packing list aur COC, aur job work ke raw material ka hisaab alag kaise rakhte hain?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Samajh gaya, heat number aur certificates ka hisaab manual hai, isse dispatch aur audit mein time lagta hai.

## 6. One line of value, specific to them (15 seconds)

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein heat-wise batch, stock, dispatch aur export docs ek jagah honge, aur Tally accounts aur GST ke liye chal sakta hai.

## Questions they will probably ask

- **Engineering: make-to-order, job costing, subcontracting, job work?** Yes: sales order to manufacturing order, subcontracting to job workers, and actual cost per job. We'll check your ITC-04 job-work reporting needs in discovery.
- **Barcodes and scanners in the store?** Yes, both support barcode scanning for receipts, picking and stock counts. Scanner hardware is extra.
- **Can you move our Tally data?** Yes. We move masters (customers, suppliers, items, ledgers) and opening balances as standard. Moving past transactions is possible but costs more, and is usually not needed.
- **How much does it cost?** Two parts: the implementation (our fee) and the software licence or hosting. Phase 1 (inventory, purchase, sales, accounting and GST) starts from ₹2 lakh for implementation. The final number depends on users, plants and how much data we migrate. We give a fixed quote after a half-day discovery.
