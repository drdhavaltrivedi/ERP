## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Behrampura aur Dholka mein se kaunsa plant bada hai, aur warehouse kahan hai?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Plant se warehouse aur customer tak lot traceability abhi kaise hoti hai, FSSC 22000 audit ke time?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Domestic aur export sales desk dono same live stock dekhte hain, ya phone karke confirm karte hain?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Toh lot traceability aur warehouse stock abhi manually jud raha hai, aur audit ke time yeh dhoondhna padta hai.

## Questions they will probably ask

- **Pharma: batch, expiry, FEFO, QC release?** Yes: lot and serial tracking with expiry dates, FEFO picking, quality checks before release, and quarantine stock. QC module availability differs by product and edition [verify for Odoo: Quality is an Enterprise app].
- **Will it pass audits? (pharma: GMP, chemicals: REACH, BRC and similar)** The system keeps a full audit trail of who changed what and when, plus lot and batch traceability from raw material to dispatch. It supports your compliance work, but certification is still your process. In pharma, say: "Batch records and QC release can be tracked in the system; we'll map it to your SOPs in discovery."
- **Multiple plants, companies or countries?** Yes. Multi-warehouse, multi-company and multi-currency are standard in both. This is one of the main reasons to move off Tally.
