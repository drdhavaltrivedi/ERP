## 2. Questions to have ready (ask one, then follow their answer)

Pick the next question from what they just said, not from the list order.

- **Easy, about their setup:** "Vatva plant mein dyes aur pigments dono bante hain, ya ek zyada?"
- **The pain question:** "Export invoice, packing list aur COA har shipment ke liye abhi haath se bante hain ya system se?"
- **Another angle:** "Alag-alag currencies mein receivables aur har lot ka shade record kaise track karte ho?"
- **Dig deeper:** "Ek shipment ke documents banane mein kitna time lagta hai?"
- **Dig deeper:** "Shade complaint aaye toh lot ka record kahan se nikalte hain?"
- **Who feels it:** "Yeh sabse zyada kisko pareshan karta hai, accounts ko, plant ko, ya aapko?"
- **The last time:** "Last time yeh hua tha, toh kya hua? Kitna time laga?"

*Echo their last few words when they go vague ("Thoda manual?"). Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

Ask one more question about it first. Then reflect it back:

> **Dhaval:** Toh export documents aur multi-currency receivables abhi haath se ho rahe hain, aur 40 countries mein yeh kaafi time leta hai. Sahi samjha?

One line of value, then the visit ask:

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein export documents sales order se, multi-currency receivables aur har lot ka shade record ek jagah. Single plant ke liye ERPNext kam kharche mein chal jaata hai, aur Tally accounts ke liye reh sakta hai. Kya main next week Vatva plant aa ke 30 minute dikha sakta hoon ki aapke setup mein yeh kaisa dikhega? Tuesday ya Thursday?

## 5. Leave with a next step

Never end with "main contact karta hoon". End with a date, a name or a number, and thank them by name:

> **Dhaval:** Bahut shukriya Haresh ji, aapka time dene ke liye. Toh main [day] ko [time] call karta hoon.

## Questions they will probably ask

- **What is the licence cost?** - Odoo: priced per user per month, billed yearly. The India Standard plan was listed around ₹725 per user per month. [verify on odoo.com/pricing]
- **Does it handle GST, e-invoicing and e-way bills?** Both have Indian localisation for GST invoices and returns data. E-invoice (IRN) and e-way bill generation through the government portals is supported. [verify: which edition and connector you deploy, and whether a GSP subscription is needed]
- **Can you move our Tally data?** Yes. We move masters (customers, suppliers, items, ledgers) and opening balances as standard. Moving past transactions is possible but costs more, and is usually not needed.
