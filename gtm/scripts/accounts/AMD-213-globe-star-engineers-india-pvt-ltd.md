## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Daskroi plant mein 50+ skilled log hain, toh production aur service team kaise coordinate hoti hai?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Pump ke spare parts aur dealer orders ka stock Odhav office aur plant mein alag track hota hai kya?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Export ke liye packing list, invoice aur certificates ka kaam manually hota hai ya system mein?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Toh spare parts aur export papers ka kaam abhi manually chal raha hai, yeh bahut time leta hoga.
