## 2. Discovery questions

1. How does an enquiry become a quote, a BOM and a work order today?
2. Do you know the actual cost of each job after it ships, or only the estimate?
3. How do you track spares, service visits and installed units at customer sites?
4. Who besides you would be involved in choosing a system: your CA, accounts head, plant head?
5. Is there a date that matters, like 1 April, a new plant, or an audit?

## 3. What to show them (their version of the value)

1. One login for all group companies, with inter-company sales and a consolidated P&L each month
2. CRM pipeline per regional sales manager, so new hires are productive from week one
3. Stock of cartridges, bags and housings by warehouse, with reorder alerts

Lead with the first point. It is the one tied to their hook.

## 4. Questions they will probably ask

- **Multiple plants, companies or countries?** Yes. Multi-warehouse, multi-company and multi-currency are standard in both. This is one of the main reasons to move off Tally.
- **How much does it cost?** Two parts: the implementation (our fee) and the software licence or hosting. Phase 1 (inventory, purchase, sales, accounting and GST) starts from ₹2 lakh for implementation. The final number depends on users, plants and how much data we migrate. We give a fixed quote after a half-day discovery.
- **Why not just use Tranzact or a Tally add-on? It's cheaper.** For one plant with simple production, it may be enough, and we'll say so. It stops being enough when you need multiple plants or companies, batch and QC records, export documentation, CRM and approvals in one system. Ask: "What still lives in Excel or WhatsApp today?"

Full answers are in `scripts/01-buyer-faq.md`. Fill the [fill] items there before calling.

## 5. Likely objections

- "Tally is enough." Agree it's good for accounts. Ask about consolidating several companies' books, which is where Tally runs out.
- "Too expensive." Phase 1 (inventory, purchase, sales, accounts, GST) starts from ₹2 lakh. Offer a fixed-scope phase 1 so cost is known up front.
- "We already use Tranzact / a Tally add-on." Ask what still lives in Excel or WhatsApp.
- "Who else uses it?" Mention a building-materials manufacturer in MP where you built the ERP (never name the client), and offer reference-deal terms.
