## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Ab group mein kitni companies ho gayi hain, aur sabse zyada growth kis product line mein hai?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Teeno-chaaron companies ka stock aur inter-company billing ek jagah dikhta hai, ya har company ki alag Tally hai?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Naye regional sales managers ka enquiry aur follow-up abhi kahan track hota hai: Excel, WhatsApp ya koi CRM?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Toh har company ki alag books hain aur month-end par consolidated P&L haath se banta hai, aur sales pipeline bhi alag-alag logon ke paas hai.

## 6. One line of value, specific to them (15 seconds)

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein saari group companies ek login mein, inter-company billing automatic, aur regional sales ka pipeline ek CRM mein. Tally chahein toh accounts ke liye chalu rakh sakte hain.

## Questions they will probably ask

- **Multiple plants, companies or countries?** Yes. Multi-warehouse, multi-company and multi-currency are standard in both. This is one of the main reasons to move off Tally.
- **How much does it cost?** Two parts: the implementation (our fee) and the software licence or hosting. Phase 1 (inventory, purchase, sales, accounting and GST) starts from ₹2 lakh for implementation. The final number depends on users, plants and how much data we migrate. We give a fixed quote after a half-day discovery.
- **Why not just use Tranzact or a Tally add-on? It's cheaper.** For one plant with simple production, it may be enough, and we'll say so. It stops being enough when you need multiple plants or companies, batch and QC records, export documentation, CRM and approvals in one system. Ask: "What still lives in Excel or WhatsApp today?"
