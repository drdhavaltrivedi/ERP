## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Kathwada plant mein machine assembly aur testing ka kaam kaise plan karte ho? Team kitni badi hai?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Machine ke spare parts ka stock aur service calls machine-wise kaise track karte ho?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Export ke liye machine ke documents, packing list aur shipment papers ek jagah kaise rakhte ho?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Toh spare parts aur export documents abhi manual chal rahe hain, ye samajh aaya.

## 6. One line of value, specific to them (15 seconds)

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein machine-wise spare parts, BOM aur export documents ek jagah rahenge, aur Tally GST aur accounts ke liye chal sakta hai.

## 7. Ask for the visit (not a demo)

> **Dhaval:** Kya main next week Kathwada plant aa ke 30 minute mil sakta hoon? Main dikhaunga ki aapke jaisa setup ek system mein kaisa dikhta hai. Tuesday ya Thursday, kaunsa theek rahega?

*Always offer two options. When they agree:* "Main aapko WhatsApp pe confirm bhej deta hoon. Yeh number sahi hai?"
