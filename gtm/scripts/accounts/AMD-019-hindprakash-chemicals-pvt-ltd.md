## 2. Questions to have ready (ask one, then follow their answer)

Pick the next question from what they just said, not from the list order.

- **Easy, about their setup:** "Paanchon plants mein se kaunse sabse bade hain, aur trading kitna hissa hai manufacturing ke comparison mein?"
- **The pain question:** "India aur teeno overseas companies ka hisaab alag currencies mein hai, toh group consolidation kaise hota hai?"
- **Another angle:** "Trading aur apni manufacturing ka margin product line ke hisaab se alag kaise nikalte hain?"
- **Dig deeper:** "Overseas companies ka month-end kitne din mein yahan aata hai?"
- **Dig deeper:** "Trading ka maal aur apna maal ek hi godown mein rehta hai?"
- **Who feels it:** "Yeh sabse zyada kisko pareshan karta hai, accounts ko, plant ko, ya aapko?"
- **The last time:** "Last time yeh hua tha, toh kya hua? Kitna time laga?"

*Echo their last few words when they go vague ("Thoda manual?"). Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 4. When you hear the pain

Ask one more question about it first. Then reflect it back:

> **Dhaval:** Toh group consolidation aur trading-manufacturing margin abhi Excel mein jodkar nikalta hai. Sahi samjha?

One line of value, then the visit ask:

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein paanchon plants aur overseas companies multi-currency mein consolidated, aur trading aur manufacturing margin alag dikhega. Tally GST ke liye reh sakta hai. Kya main next week Vatva plant aa ke 30 minute dikha sakta hoon ki aapke setup mein yeh kaisa dikhega? Tuesday ya Thursday?

*When they agree:* "Main WhatsApp pe confirm bhejta hoon. Yeh number sahi hai?"

## 5. Leave with a next step

Never end with "main contact karta hoon". End with a date, a name or a number, and thank them by name:

> **Dhaval:** Bahut shukriya Sanjay ji, aapka time dene ke liye. Toh main [day] ko [time] call karta hoon.
