## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Aapki range mein single screw se parallel twin screw tak sab hai, production team mein kitne log kaam karte hain?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Har screw aur barrel ka drawing, material grade aur heat treatment ka batch record kaise rakhte hain, abhi register mein?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Export order ke liye packing list, invoice aur dispatch documents ek saath kaise banate hain?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back
