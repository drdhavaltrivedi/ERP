Pick the next question from what they just said, not from the list order.

- **Easy, about their setup:** "Bavla plant mein Ayurvedic hair oil aur soap ki line kitni badi hai, aur team kitni hai?"
- **The pain question:** "Ek hi product ki kai batch chal rahi ho, tab batch aur expiry aap kaise track karte ho?"
- **Another angle:** "Third party manufacturing ke liye alag clients ka BOM aur raw material stock ek jagah kaise dikhte hain?"
- **Dig deeper:** "Yeh kaam abhi kaun sambhalta hai, aur kitni baar?"
- **Dig deeper:** "Yeh hamesha se aisa hai, ya naya plant ya naya product aane ke baad shuru hua?"
- **Who feels it:** "Yeh sabse zyada kisko pareshan karta hai, accounts ko, plant ko, ya aapko?"
- **The last time:** "Last time yeh hua tha, toh kya hua? Kitna time laga?"

*Echo their last few words when they go vague ("Thoda manual?"). Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 4. When you hear the pain

Ask one more question about it first. Then reflect it back:

> **Dhaval:** Toh batch aur third party ka stock abhi manual sambhal rahe ho, isliye dispatch aur expiry mein risk rehta hai. Sahi samjha?

One line of value, then the visit ask:

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein batch, expiry, BOM aur dispatch ek screen par dikhenge, aur Tally accounts aur GST ke liye rakh sakte ho. Kya main next week Bavla plant aa ke 30 minute dikha sakta hoon ki aapke setup mein yeh kaisa dikhega? Tuesday ya Thursday?

*When they agree:* "Main WhatsApp pe confirm bhejta hoon. Yeh number sahi hai?"

## 5. Leave with a next step

Never end with "main contact karta hoon". End with a date, a name or a number, and thank them by name:

> **Dhaval:** Bahut shukriya Atul ji, aapka time dene ke liye. Toh main [day] ko [time] call karta hoon.

## Questions they will probably ask

- **Can you move our Tally data?** Yes. We move masters (customers, suppliers, items, ledgers) and opening balances as standard. Moving past transactions is possible but costs more, and is usually not needed.
- **Can we keep using Tally for accounts?** Yes, for a transition period. Your CA can keep filing from Tally while operations run in Odoo or ERPNext. Most clients move accounts over at the start of a financial year.
- **How much does it cost?** Two parts: the implementation (our fee) and the software licence or hosting. Phase 1 (inventory, purchase, sales, accounting and GST) starts from ₹2 lakh for implementation. The final number depends on users, plants and how much data we migrate. We give a fixed quote after a half-day discovery.
- **Does it handle GST, e-invoicing and e-way bills?** Both have Indian localisation for GST invoices and returns data. E-invoice (IRN) and e-way bill generation through the government portals is supported. [verify: which edition and connector you deploy, and whether a GSP subscription is needed]
- **Mobile app? Can the owner see numbers on the phone?** Yes. Both have mobile access, so the owner can see sales, stock, receivables and approvals on the phone.
