## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Dono units mein kaunse products bante hain, agitators, pumps ya dono?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Ek enquiry se quote, BOM aur work order tak ka safar abhi kaise chalta hai: system mein ya Excel aur email mein?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Order ship hone ke baad actual job cost pata chalta hai, ya sirf estimate rehta hai?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Toh quote se BOM aur job costing abhi Excel mein hai, aur regional offices ko order status ke liye phone karna padta hai.

## 6. One line of value, specific to them (15 seconds)

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein enquiry se quote, BOM, work order aur actual job cost ek flow mein, aur saare offices same status dekhte hain. Tally accounts ke liye reh sakta hai.

## 7. Ask for the visit (not a demo)

> **Dhaval:** Kya main next week Vatva unit aa ke 30 minute mil sakta hoon? Main dikhaunga ki aapke jaisa setup ek system mein kaisa dikhta hai. Tuesday ya Thursday, kaunsa theek rahega?

*Always offer two options. When they agree:* "Main aapko WhatsApp pe confirm bhej deta hoon. Yeh number sahi hai?"

- **Engineering: make-to-order, job costing, subcontracting, job work?** Yes: sales order to manufacturing order, subcontracting to job workers, and actual cost per job. We'll check your ITC-04 job-work reporting needs in discovery.

- **Can you customise it for our process?** Yes, but we try to use standard features first, because standard features are cheaper to maintain and upgrade. Customisations are quoted separately.
