## 2. Discovery questions

1. How does an enquiry become a quote, a BOM and a work order today?
2. Do you know the actual cost of each job after it ships, or only the estimate?
3. How do you track spares, service visits and installed units at customer sites?
4. Who besides you would be involved in choosing a system: your CA, accounts head, plant head?
5. Is there a date that matters, like 1 April, a new plant, or an audit?

## 3. What to show them (their version of the value)

1. Spares inventory and service history per pump sold in 50+ countries
2. Separate sales, spares and service desks working from one customer record
3. Thaltej head office and Pirana plant in one system

Lead with the first point. It is the one tied to their hook.

## 4. Questions they will probably ask

- **Engineering: make-to-order, job costing, subcontracting, job work?** Yes: sales order to manufacturing order, subcontracting to job workers, and actual cost per job. We'll check your ITC-04 job-work reporting needs in discovery.
- **Mobile app? Can the owner see numbers on the phone?** Yes. Both have mobile access, so the owner can see sales, stock, receivables and approvals on the phone.
- **What is the licence cost?** - Odoo: priced per user per month, billed yearly. The India Standard plan was listed around ₹725 per user per month. [verify on odoo.com/pricing]

## 5. Likely objections

- "Tally is enough." Agree it's good for accounts. Ask about spares inventory and service history per installed pump, which is where Tally runs out.
- "Too expensive." Phase 1 (inventory, purchase, sales, accounts, GST) starts from ₹2 lakh. ERPNext has no per-user licence if budget is tight.
- "We already use Tranzact / a Tally add-on." Ask what still lives in Excel or WhatsApp.
- "Who else uses it?" Mention a building-materials manufacturer in MP where you built the ERP (never name the client), and offer reference-deal terms.
