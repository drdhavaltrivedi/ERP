## 2. Questions to have ready (ask one, then follow their answer)

Pick the next question from what they just said, not from the list order.

- **Easy, about their setup:** "Pirana plant mein kaunse pumps sabse zyada bante hain, aur export kitna hissa hai?"
- **The pain question:** "Har pump jo bika hai, uska spares aur service history ek jagah dikhta hai, ya teeno desks ka alag record hai?"
- **Another angle:** "Thaltej office aur Pirana plant ke beech stock aur order status kaise sync hota hai?"
- **Dig deeper:** "Customer spares ke liye call kare toh model aur serial number kaise dhoondhte hain?"
- **Dig deeper:** "Export orders ke documents kaun banata hai?"
- **Who feels it:** "Yeh sabse zyada kisko pareshan karta hai, accounts ko, plant ko, ya aapko?"
- **The last time:** "Last time yeh hua tha, toh kya hua? Kitna time laga?"

*Echo their last few words when they go vague ("Thoda manual?"). Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 4. When you hear the pain

Ask one more question about it first. Then reflect it back:

> **Dhaval:** Toh sales, spares aur service ka record alag-alag desk par hai, aur customer ki poori history ek jagah nahi milti. Sahi samjha?

One line of value, then the visit ask:

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein har pump ka spares aur service history, aur teeno desks ek customer record se kaam karte hain. Tally accounts ke liye reh sakta hai. Kya main next week Pirana plant aa ke 30 minute dikha sakta hoon ki aapke setup mein yeh kaisa dikhega? Tuesday ya Thursday?

*When they agree:* "Main WhatsApp pe confirm bhejta hoon. Yeh number sahi hai?"

## 5. Leave with a next step

Never end with "main contact karta hoon". End with a date, a name or a number, and thank them by name:

> **Dhaval:** Bahut shukriya Vishal ji, aapka time dene ke liye. Toh main [day] ko [time] call karta hoon.
