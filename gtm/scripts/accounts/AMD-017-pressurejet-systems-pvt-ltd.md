## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Pirana plant mein kaunse pumps sabse zyada bante hain, aur export kitna hissa hai?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Har pump jo bika hai, uska spares aur service history ek jagah dikhta hai, ya teeno desks ka alag record hai?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Thaltej office aur Pirana plant ke beech stock aur order status kaise sync hota hai?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Toh sales, spares aur service ka record alag-alag desk par hai, aur customer ki poori history ek jagah nahi milti.

## 6. One line of value, specific to them (15 seconds)

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein har pump ka spares aur service history, aur teeno desks ek customer record se kaam karte hain. Tally accounts ke liye reh sakta hai.

## 7. Ask for the visit (not a demo)

> **Dhaval:** Kya main next week Pirana plant aa ke 30 minute mil sakta hoon? Main dikhaunga ki aapke jaisa setup ek system mein kaisa dikhta hai. Tuesday ya Thursday, kaunsa theek rahega?

*Always offer two options. When they agree:* "Main aapko WhatsApp pe confirm bhej deta hoon. Yeh number sahi hai?"
