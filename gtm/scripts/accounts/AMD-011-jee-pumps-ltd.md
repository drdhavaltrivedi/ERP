## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Foundry aur machining ek hi campus mein hai, ya teeno campus ka kaam alag hai?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Ek pump model ka foundry se machining aur assembly tak ka actual cost abhi kaise nikalte hain?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Teeno campus ka stock aur work orders ek jagah dikhte hain, ya har campus ka alag register?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Toh foundry se assembly tak ka costing aur teeno campus ka stock abhi alag-alag jagah hai.

## 6. One line of value, specific to them (15 seconds)

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein har pump model ka foundry, machining aur assembly cost, aur teeno campus ka stock aur work orders ek view mein. Tally accounts aur GST ke liye reh sakta hai.

## 7. Ask for the visit (not a demo)

> **Dhaval:** Kya main next week Odhav plant aa ke 30 minute mil sakta hoon? Main dikhaunga ki aapke jaisa setup ek system mein kaisa dikhta hai. Tuesday ya Thursday, kaunsa theek rahega?

*Always offer two options. When they agree:* "Main aapko WhatsApp pe confirm bhej deta hoon. Yeh number sahi hai?"

## Questions they will probably ask

- **Engineering: make-to-order, job costing, subcontracting, job work?** Yes: sales order to manufacturing order, subcontracting to job workers, and actual cost per job. We'll check your ITC-04 job-work reporting needs in discovery.
- **Will it pass audits? (pharma: GMP, chemicals: REACH, BRC and similar)** The system keeps a full audit trail of who changed what and when, plus lot and batch traceability from raw material to dispatch. It supports your compliance work, but certification is still your process. In pharma, say: "Batch records and QC release can be tracked in the system; we'll map it to your SOPs in discovery."
- **How long will it take?** Phase 1 typically takes 8 to 12 weeks from kickoff to go-live, depending on how quickly your team gives us data and decisions. [fill: confirm from your own delivery plan] Manufacturing, QC and costing come in phase 2.
