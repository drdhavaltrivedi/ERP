## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Chhatral factory mein machine assembly aur clean room projects ki team kitni badi hai?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Machine ke spare parts aur service calls ka record abhi kaise rakhte ho, warranty ke saath?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Project costing aur machine BOM, jaise SS parts aur HVAC components, abhi kaise track karte ho?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 5. Listen for the pain, then reflect it back

If they mention Excel, WhatsApp, registers, phoning the plant or month-end delays:

> **Dhaval:** Toh spare parts aur project costing ka hisaab manual chal raha hai, isliye service mein deri ho sakti hai.

## 6. One line of value, specific to them (15 seconds)

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein machine BOM, spare parts stock aur project costing ek jagah aa jayenge, aur Tally accounts aur GST ke liye waise hi chal sakta hai.
