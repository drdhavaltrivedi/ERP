## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Mundra plant aur Ahmedabad office ke beech production planning kaise hoti hai?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Export ke liye batch-wise COA aur jumbo bag ka stock kaise track karte hain?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Alag grade ke ATH ka batch aur customer order ek system mein kaise link hota hai?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## Questions they will probably ask

- **Chemicals: recipes, yield, by-products, multiple units of measure?** Yes. A bill of materials can produce by-products, and you can record actual yield against standard. You can buy in kg and sell in drums or tankers.
- **Multiple plants, companies or countries?** Yes. Multi-warehouse, multi-company and multi-currency are standard in both. This is one of the main reasons to move off Tally.
- **Can you move our Tally data?** Yes. We move masters (customers, suppliers, items, ledgers) and opening balances as standard. Moving past transactions is possible but costs more, and is usually not needed.
- **How much does it cost?** Two parts: the implementation (our fee) and the software licence or hosting. Phase 1 (inventory, purchase, sales, accounting and GST) starts from ₹2 lakh for implementation. The final number depends on users, plants and how much data we migrate. We give a fixed quote after a half-day discovery.
- **Does it handle GST, e-invoicing and e-way bills?** Both have Indian localisation for GST invoices and returns data. E-invoice (IRN) and e-way bill generation through the government portals is supported. [verify: which edition and connector you deploy, and whether a GSP subscription is needed]
