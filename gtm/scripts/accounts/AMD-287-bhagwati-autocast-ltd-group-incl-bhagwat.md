## 4. Easy questions first (two-way, 1 to 2 minutes)

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. Repeat back one detail before the next question.

1. **Their role (easy):** "Aap khud operations side dekhte hain ya finance side?"
2. **Their setup (easy, flattering):** "Rajoda aur Rinal Machining ka kaam kaise coordinate karte ho, production team kitni badi hai?"
3. **Current system (easy):** "Abhi billing aur stock ke liye Tally use hota hai ya koi aur software?"
4. **Opens the pain:** "Castings ke heat ya pour batch ka record machining ke baad piece-wise kaise trace karte ho?"
5. **Opens the pain, another angle:** "Tractor parts ke liye job work aur customer-wise dispatch, dono ka record kaise rakhte ho?"

*Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*
