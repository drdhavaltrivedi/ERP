## 2. Questions to have ready (ask one, then follow their answer)

Pick the next question from what they just said, not from the list order.

- **Easy, about their setup:** "Naroda plant mein kaunse pump models sabse zyada bante hain, aur export abhi kitna hai?"
- **The pain question:** "Export enquiry se quote aur dispatch tak ka kaam abhi kaise chalta hai: Excel aur email mein ya system mein?"
- **Another angle:** "Spares aur service desk har pump ka record kaise rakhta hai, aur dealer orders ka credit kaise dekhte hain?"
- **Dig deeper:** "Export quote banane mein kitna time lagta hai, aur kaun banata hai?"
- **Dig deeper:** "Dealer ka credit limit cross ho toh kaun rokta hai?"
- **Who feels it:** "Yeh sabse zyada kisko pareshan karta hai, accounts ko, plant ko, ya aapko?"
- **The last time:** "Last time yeh hua tha, toh kya hua? Kitna time laga?"

*Echo their last few words when they go vague ("Thoda manual?"). Don't ask about revenue or margins on the first call.*

## 4. When you hear the pain

Ask one more question about it first. Then reflect it back:

> **Dhaval:** Toh export quotes aur spares-service record abhi alag files mein hain, aur naye export manager ko yeh sab dhoondhna padega. Sahi samjha?

One line of value, then the visit ask:

> **Dhaval:** Hum yahi karte hain. Ek ERP mein export enquiry se dispatch tak, spares aur service ek customer record par, aur dealer credit control. Tally accounts ke liye reh sakta hai. Kya main next week Naroda plant aa ke 30 minute dikha sakta hoon ki aapke setup mein yeh kaisa dikhega? Tuesday ya Thursday?

*When they agree:* "Main WhatsApp pe confirm bhejta hoon. Yeh number sahi hai?"

## 5. Leave with a next step

Never end with "main contact karta hoon". End with a date, a name or a number, and thank them by name:

> **Dhaval:** Bahut shukriya Parth ji, aapka time dene ke liye. Toh main [day] ko [time] call karta hoon.
