# Buyer FAQ: questions prospects will ask, and how to answer

Use this on calls, on visits and when replying to emails. The answers are short on purpose: say the first line, then stop and let them ask more.

Labels:
- **[fill]**: your own company fact that only you can supply (team size, office address, support hours, AMC price). Fill these in before the first call.
- **[verify]**: product or pricing detail that changes often. Check the vendor's current page before you quote it.

The top 10 questions have a Gujarati version at the end.

## A. About you

**1. Who are you, and why should we trust a new partner?**
We are an official partner for both Odoo and ERPNext, based in [fill: city/office]. We built an ERP for a building-materials manufacturer in Madhya Pradesh. We're taking on two or three Ahmedabad manufacturers this year on reference terms: a fixed price in return for a case study once you're live.
*Never name the MP client. If they press: "They asked us to keep their name private. I can arrange a call with them if we get to a proposal."* Only offer that call if the client has agreed to it.

**2. Who else in our industry uses Odoo or ERPNext?**
Both are used by manufacturers worldwide, including pharma, chemicals and engineering firms. In Ahmedabad, you'd be one of our first references, and that's why we're offering reference terms. Be honest here. Do not claim local customers you don't have.

**3. How big is your team? What happens if the person handling us leaves?**
[fill: team size and roles]. Every project has a named lead and a second person who knows the setup. All configuration is documented and handed over to you.

**4. Why both Odoo and ERPNext? Isn't that confusing?**
It lets us recommend what fits you instead of pushing one product. Odoo is more polished and covers more areas: CRM, website, quality, helpdesk. ERPNext is open source with no per-user licence, so it's cheaper to run and easier to customise. We tell you which one we'd pick for you, and why.

## B. Cost

**5. How much does it cost?**
Two parts: the implementation (our fee) and the software licence or hosting. Phase 1 (inventory, purchase, sales, accounting and GST) starts from ₹2 lakh for implementation. The final number depends on users, plants and how much data we migrate. We give a fixed quote after a half-day discovery.

**6. What is the licence cost?**
- Odoo: priced per user per month, billed yearly. The India Standard plan was listed around ₹725 per user per month. [verify on odoo.com/pricing]
- ERPNext: the software is free. You pay for hosting (Frappe Cloud starts around ₹410 a month [verify]) or run it on your own server.
- With Tally, you also keep or drop the Tally licence. Many clients keep Tally for a year as a backup.

**7. Is there any hidden cost?**
Possible extras: more users later, custom reports or custom features beyond the agreed scope, hardware (barcode scanners, label printers), and the annual support contract. Everything is listed in the quote.

**8. What is the annual support or AMC cost?**
[fill: AMC structure, e.g. a percentage of implementation or a fixed monthly fee, plus hours included].

**9. Can we pay in stages?**
Yes. Typically 30-40% at kickoff, then on milestones (configuration done, data migrated, go-live). [fill: confirm your standard terms]

**10. Why not just use Tranzact or a Tally add-on? It's cheaper.**
For one plant with simple production, it may be enough, and we'll say so. It stops being enough when you need multiple plants or companies, batch and QC records, export documentation, CRM and approvals in one system. Ask: "What still lives in Excel or WhatsApp today?"

## C. Implementation

**11. How long will it take?**
Phase 1 typically takes 8 to 12 weeks from kickoff to go-live, depending on how quickly your team gives us data and decisions. [fill: confirm from your own delivery plan] Manufacturing, QC and costing come in phase 2.

**12. How much of our team's time does it need?**
One owner-side decision maker for an hour a week, one key user per department for a few hours a week during setup, and full-day training sessions before go-live.

**13. Will our business stop during the switch?**
No. We run the new system alongside the old one for one or two weeks before go-live, so you can compare the numbers. Many clients go live on 1 April or at a month start, so the books open clean.

**14. What if we don't like it after go-live?**
Phase 1 has a defined scope and acceptance criteria, which we agree in writing. Your data is always exportable. You are not locked in, especially with ERPNext, which is open source.

**15. Can you customise it for our process?**
Yes, but we try to use standard features first, because standard features are cheaper to maintain and upgrade. Customisations are quoted separately.

## D. Data, Tally and migration

**16. Can you move our Tally data?**
Yes. We move masters (customers, suppliers, items, ledgers) and opening balances as standard. Moving past transactions is possible but costs more, and is usually not needed.

**17. Can we keep using Tally for accounts?**
Yes, for a transition period. Your CA can keep filing from Tally while operations run in Odoo or ERPNext. Most clients move accounts over at the start of a financial year.

**18. Our CA only knows Tally. Will they be able to work with this?**
We give your CA their own login and the standard reports they need: trial balance, ledgers, GST reports. We're happy to walk them through it in one session.

**19. Is our data safe? Who can see it?**
Each user sees only what their role allows. You can host on a cloud server in India, or on your own server at the office. You own the data and can export it any time.

**20. Does it work without internet?**
Both are web-based. With your own server in the office, it runs over the local network even when the internet is down. Cloud hosting needs an internet connection.

## E. GST and compliance

**21. Does it handle GST, e-invoicing and e-way bills?**
Both have Indian localisation for GST invoices and returns data. E-invoice (IRN) and e-way bill generation through the government portals is supported. [verify: which edition and connector you deploy, and whether a GSP subscription is needed]

**22. TDS and TCS?**
Supported in the Indian localisation. [verify for the version you deploy]

**23. Will it pass audits? (pharma: GMP, chemicals: REACH, BRC and similar)**
The system keeps a full audit trail of who changed what and when, plus lot and batch traceability from raw material to dispatch. It supports your compliance work, but certification is still your process. In pharma, say: "Batch records and QC release can be tracked in the system; we'll map it to your SOPs in discovery."

## F. Features by industry

**24. Pharma: batch, expiry, FEFO, QC release?**
Yes: lot and serial tracking with expiry dates, FEFO picking, quality checks before release, and quarantine stock. QC module availability differs by product and edition [verify for Odoo: Quality is an Enterprise app].

**25. Chemicals: recipes, yield, by-products, multiple units of measure?**
Yes. A bill of materials can produce by-products, and you can record actual yield against standard. You can buy in kg and sell in drums or tankers.

**26. Engineering: make-to-order, job costing, subcontracting, job work?**
Yes: sales order to manufacturing order, subcontracting to job workers, and actual cost per job. We'll check your ITC-04 job-work reporting needs in discovery.

**27. Multiple plants, companies or countries?**
Yes. Multi-warehouse, multi-company and multi-currency are standard in both. This is one of the main reasons to move off Tally.

**28. Mobile app? Can the owner see numbers on the phone?**
Yes. Both have mobile access, so the owner can see sales, stock, receivables and approvals on the phone.

**29. Barcodes and scanners in the store?**
Yes, both support barcode scanning for receipts, picking and stock counts. Scanner hardware is extra.

## Questions you should ask back

When they ask a question, answer briefly, then ask one of these:
- "What made you ask that? Has it been a problem before?"
- "Who else would need to be comfortable with this: your CA, accounts head, plant head?"
- "If this works, when would you want to go live?"
